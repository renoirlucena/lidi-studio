The first portrait of a new life, painted in light.

ABOUT THE STUDIO
She paints with light.
She paints with oil.
/lee-dee/
is a fine art photographer based in Anchorage, Alaska. Twenty years behind the camera. A lifetime behind the easel.
Her practice as an oil painter informs every frame: composition is built, not captured; light is layered, not found; drapery is sculpted on the dressform before it ever meets the model. The photograph is the last gesture, not the first.
The studio composes a small number of commissions each season.
Maternity, newborn, motherhood, family
— each rendered as a portrait, not a session.
