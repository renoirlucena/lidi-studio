About

Lidi.

A photographer's practice, shaped by a painter's training.

I'm Lidi. I trained as a painter before I picked up a camera, and that training still shapes the work. Every session I do is composed the way I'd compose a painting — slow light, considered wardrobe, careful attention to how a body moves through space.

Originally from Brazil, now based in Anchorage, Alaska. I photograph maternity, motherhood, newborn, and family — work that takes time, made for keeping.
