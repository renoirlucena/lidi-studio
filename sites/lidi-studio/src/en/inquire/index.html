FREQUENTLY ASKED
Do you publish session prices?

Each session is custom. Once I understand what you're hoping for — maternity, newborn, family, or a sequence — I'll send current investment levels and availability.

Where do you photograph?

My studio is in Anchorage. I also work on location across South-Central Alaska — Eagle River, Palmer, Wasilla, Girdwood.

When should I book a maternity session?

For maternity, late second trimester is the sweet spot. For newborn, within the first two weeks. Family and motherhood: any time. Inquire early — calendars fill up.

What should I wear?

We choose your wardrobe together. I have a small studio collection of dresses and drapery, and I'll guide you on what to bring.

How long does a session take?

It varies by session type. We talk first, work slowly, take the time the work needs. I'll share specifics when we connect.

How long until I receive my photos?

Inquire for current delivery timeline. Each portrait is composed and finished individually, which takes time.

Do you photograph weddings or commercial work?

Not currently. Lidi Studio is dedicated to portraiture — maternity, motherhood, newborn, family.

Do you offer prints?

Yes. Fine art prints, archival albums, and framed work are available. We discuss this when you receive your gallery.
