## 7. Realistic timeline

| Month | Expected signals |
|---|---|
| Month 1–2 | Foundation built; GMB live; first reviews |
| Month 3–4 | First long-tails ranking page 2–3 |
| Month 5–6 | Long-tails ranking page 1 |
| Month 12+ | Competing for mainstream keywords (`fine art photography anchorage`) |

A high Lighthouse score does not produce ranking. It removes a friction.
The wins come from: (a) consistent on-topic content (the 12-post calendar);
(b) a healthy GMB profile with reviews; (c) a few well-placed backlinks.
