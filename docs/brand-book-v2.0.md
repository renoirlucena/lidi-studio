## Why this version was deprecated

The v2.0 palette (Carbon Black `#1A1612` dominant + Bone `#E8DFCF` secondary + Oxblood `#5A1E1E` accent) tested **funereal** when rendered against the actual Lidi Studio subject matter — maternity, newborn, family. The dark-dominant register read as European fashion gallery, not as a place a pregnant Anchorage / JBER woman wants to imagine herself. This was the catalyst for v2.1 (light-register revision) and ultimately v2.2 (Sargent Luminous).

Specific failure modes recorded for institutional memory:
- A pregnant woman looking at the Carbon Black homepage felt asked to perform aesthetic literacy before being allowed in.
- Oxblood as accent read as dried blood / funereal Renaissance against the maternal subject.
- The military spouse audience responded to *gravity without coldness*; v2.0 was cold.
- Maternity-red dresses in photographs were lost in shadow against Carbon Black, instead of gaining vibrancy by contrast.

### Studio activation rules *(unchanged through v2.2)*
1. **Copy:** "studio" never appears as a service descriptor. Required pairings: *the studio · studio practice · the work coming out of the studio · in studio · studio commission*. Painter-vocabulary word required within 7 words.
2. **Visual:** STUDIO in wordmark renders only in Cormorant Garamond caps tracked. Never sans. Never adjacent to commercial UI.
3. **Behavioral:** activities are *commissions* and *sittings* (not *sessions* / *packages*); outputs are *portraits, prints, archive*; verbs are *composed, rendered, sculpted*.
