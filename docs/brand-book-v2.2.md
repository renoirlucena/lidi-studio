## 4. Logo system

### 4.1 Primary mark — Hermès-style integrated wordmark

```
   L I D I
```

**A single integrated wordmark, not stacked, not separated by a divider.** The brand name is one word, set in Cormorant Garamond, with the **L** rendered in **Aged Gold `#B89968`** and **IDI** rendered in **Warm Charcoal `#4A3F38`** (or full charcoal in mono contexts). Generous letter-spacing (~+0.18em). No "STUDIO" subline in the primary mark — the descriptor lives in copy, not in the logo.

This is the **Hermès model**: the brand is the name, set with conviction and tracking, with no floreios. No swashes. No drop caps. No frames. No tagline beneath. The dignity is in the restraint.

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Font | Cormorant Garamond, Medium 500, ALL CAPS |
| Letter-spacing | +0.18em |
| L color (canonical) | `#B89968` Aged Gold |
| IDI color (canonical) | `#4A3F38` Warm Charcoal |
| Mono variants | Full Warm Charcoal `#4A3F38` (on light) · Full Champagne `#F5EBDD` (on charcoal) · Full Aged Gold `#B89968` (gilded ceremonial) |

### 4.2 Configurations

| # | Variation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two-tone wordmark** (Gold L + Charcoal IDI) on Champagne Cream | **Canonical default** — site nav, hero, papelaria |
| 2 | Full Warm Charcoal wordmark on Champagne Cream | Mono contexts where two-tone is unavailable |
| 3 | Full Champagne Cream wordmark on Warm Charcoal | Reserved — premium PDF cover, archival portfolio back |
| 4 | Full Aged Gold wordmark on Warm Charcoal | Ceremonial — Triptych portfolio book, certificates |
| 5 | L monogram (single Aged Gold letter, Cormorant Italic) | Favicon · Instagram icon · watermark on light prints · deboss seal |
| 6 | L monogram in Champagne on Charcoal | Watermark on dark prints |
| 7 | L monogram in solid Aged Gold disc — wax-seal mark | Print certificate · proposal cover · ceremonial pieces only |

### 4.3 Clear space and minimums
- Clear space = height of the letter "I" of LIDI on all sides
- Wordmark minimum: 120px digital · 25mm print
- L monogram minimum: 24px digital · 8mm print

### 4.4 What the logo does NOT do
- No tagline integrated into the mark
- No "STUDIO" appended in the wordmark itself
- No hairline divider between letters
- No drop cap treatment
- No swash variants
- No floreios — the Hermès rule is *restraint as authority*

## 5. Studio activation rules

The word **"Studio"** can read as fine art atelier *or* yoga studio. Three rules lock the reading toward "art studio".

### Rule 1 — Copy
"Studio" never appears as a service descriptor. **Forbidden:** *studio session · studio package · studio rates · studio shoot.* **Required pairings:** *the studio · studio practice · the work coming out of the studio · in studio · studio commission.* Within any sentence containing "studio", at least one painter-vocabulary word (*compose, render, painted, sculpted, drape, atmosphere, oil, pigment, sitting, commission*) must appear within 7 words.

### Rule 2 — Visual
"STUDIO" — when it appears in copy — sits inside editorial sentences with painterly imagery proximate. It never sits adjacent to commercial UI (buttons, price chips, quantity selectors, e-commerce widgets). When set typographically, it uses Cormorant Garamond caps tracked, never sans-serif.

### Rule 3 — Behavioral
The studio is named as an art practice in every operational moment. **Activities** are *commissions* and *sittings* — not *sessions* or *packages*. **Outputs** are *portraits, prints, archive* — not *deliverables* or *files*. **Process verbs** are *composed, rendered, sculpted* — not *shot, snapped, captured*. **Investment language** stays editorial: *"Investment from $X"* or *"By commission only"* — never *"package starting at"* or *"pricing tiers"*.

## 7. Palette — **Sargent Luminous** *(LOCKED)*

### 7.1 The principle

A literal Sargent palette: warm Champagne Cream walls, soft mauve in the skin tones, pearl grey in the atmospheric distances, gilded gold in the picture-frame moments, and warm charcoal as the only deep — and only for type. Light wins. Shadow serves the light. The pregnant Anchorage / JBER woman is invited *into* a luminous atelier, not staged against a black wall.

### 7.2 The five colors

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role | % |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Champagne Cream** | `#F5EBDD` | 245, 235, 221 | **Primary** — dominant background, the light register | **60%** |
| **Soft Mauve** | `#D4B5B0` | 212, 181, 176 | **Secondary** — skin-warm accents, soft sections, hover states | **20%** |
| **Pearl Grey** | `#C9C2B8` | 201, 194, 184 | **Atmospheric** — backgrounds variation, dividers, ambient | **12%** |
| **Aged Gold** | `#B89968` | 184, 153, 104 | **Signature accent** — wordmark *L*, CTAs, gilded rules, wax seal | **5%** |
| **Warm Charcoal** | `#4A3F38` | 74, 63, 56 | **Type only** — body, headlines, never as dominant surface | **3%** |

### 7.3 Proportion rules

- **60% Champagne Cream** — the brand's everyday surface. Home, Work, Commissions, About, Journal index — all default to Champagne.
- **20% Soft Mauve** — the warmth that v2.1 lacked. Soft-section backgrounds, gentle accents, hover state color shifts, secondary surface variation. Adds skin-warmth that harmonizes with maternal photographs.
- **12% Pearl Grey** — the atmospheric distance. Dividers between sections, ambient borders, the soft *negative space color*.
- **5% Aged Gold** — every gilded moment. The wordmark's L. Primary CTA underlines. Wax seal. Hover state on serif links. Frames around print certificates.
- **3% Warm Charcoal** — type and only type. Body, headlines, footer dark slabs (sparing). Never dominates a surface.

### 7.4 Pairings and contrast

| Foreground | Background | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm Charcoal `#4A3F38` | Champagne Cream `#F5EBDD` | Default body & headlines | ~10:1 (AAA) |
| Aged Gold `#B89968` | Champagne Cream | The L · CTAs · accent rules | ~3:1 — *decorative / large only* |
| Warm Charcoal | Soft Mauve `#D4B5B0` | Type on soft sections | ~6.5:1 (AA) |
| Champagne Cream | Warm Charcoal | Type on dark ceremonial sections | ~10:1 (AAA) |
| Aged Gold | Warm Charcoal | Wordmark on dark surfaces | ~4:1 (AA large) |

CTAs that need AA-grade text contrast use Warm Charcoal text with an Aged Gold underline rule, not Aged Gold as the body color of the link.

### 7.5 What this palette refuses
- Pure black anywhere (only Warm Charcoal as the deepest tone — and only at 3%, only for type)
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` (page background is always Champagne Cream)
- Cool blue-grey at any volume
- Dried-blood reds, oxblood, burgundy
- Pinterest beige (Champagne Cream leads with saturation and pigment honesty)

### 7.6 Why this palette wins

A pregnant Anchorage woman looking at the Carbon Black v2.0 palette walked into a black-walled European fashion gallery and felt asked to perform aesthetic literacy before being allowed in. The Sargent Luminous palette builds the room she actually wants to walk into: a luminous atelier with northern light through tall windows, warm cream walls, soft mauve drapery, gilded picture frames, walnut beams faintly visible. Soft Mauve at 20% is the difference — it's the skin-warm tone that v2.1 lacked entirely, and it carries breath through every section. Aged Gold is brighter, more gilded, more *frame-of-a-painting* than Aged Brass; it lifts the brand from craft-trade to art-house. Warm Charcoal as type-only at 3% lets the brand feel inhabited by people, not curated by an institution. The brand still says *fine art*. It stops saying *and you must earn the right to be here*.

## 8. Typography

| Layer | Font | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Display (primary)** | Cormorant Garamond | Google Fonts (free) |
| **Display (hairline / ceremonial)** | Italiana | Google Fonts (free) |
| **Body / secondary display** | EB Garamond | Google Fonts (free) |

| **Mono (eyebrows / captions / hex / dates)** | JetBrains Mono | Google Fonts (free) |

All five families are free Google Fonts. Production builds self-host via `@font-face`, subset to Latin + Latin-Extended.

### Hierarchy *(reference; see `/mockups/claude-web/lidi-studio-homepage.html` for live spec)*

| Level | Font | Size (desktop) | Weight | Tracking | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Masthead / H0 | Cormorant Garamond | clamp(60–144px) | 500 | -0.018em | Sentence |
| H1 | Cormorant Garamond | clamp(42–88px) | 500 | -0.012em | Sentence |
| H2 (italic) | Cormorant Garamond Italic | clamp(34–68px) | 400 italic | 0 | Sentence |
| H3 | Cormorant Garamond | clamp(24–32px) | 500 | -0.005em | Sentence |
| Eyebrow | JetBrains Mono | 11–12px | 400 | +0.22em | ALL CAPS |
| Lede | EB Garamond | 20–22px | 400 | 0 | Sentence |
| Body | EB Garamond | 16–17px | 400 | 0 | Sentence |
| Caption / footnote | JetBrains Mono | 10–12px | 400 | +0.12em | ALL CAPS |
| Pull quote (hairline) | Italiana | clamp(28–56px) | 400 | 0 | Sentence |
| Inline pron cue | JetBrains Mono | 11px | 400 | +0.12em | LEE-DEE |

### Pairing rules
- Cormorant Garamond reserved for headlines, mastheads, the wordmark, italic display moments
- Italiana reserved for ceremonial hairline moments — pull quotes, manifesto eyebrows, single-purpose ceremonial text
- EB Garamond is the body workhorse (paired with Cormorant for editorial register)
- Inter is for tiny mono labels only — never body
- JetBrains Mono for eyebrows, captions, hex codes, dates, pronunciation cues

## 10. Commission architecture

```
                    LIDI · STUDIO
                    ─────────────
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
       │                  │                  │
   THE BECOMING       FIRST LIGHT       THE MOTHERING
   (Maternity)        (Newborn)         (Motherhood)
       │                  │                  │
       └──────────────────┼──────────────────┘
                          │
                  ╔═══════╧════════╗
                  ║  THE TRIPTYCH   ║
                  ╚═════════════════╝
                  flagship · 4 / year

                          +
                     THE HEARTH
                  (Family · entry)
```

### Five commissions *(descriptions match `/mockups/claude-web/lidi-studio-homepage.html`)*

#### 01 · The Hearth — Family · Entry
> *A composed family portrait, in the painter's tradition.*
>
> Ninety minutes in studio or on location. Direction of figurine, sculpted light, and a small archive of fifteen rendered portraits.

#### 02 · The Becoming — Maternity · Signature
> *A composed portrait of the woman becoming a mother.*
>
> Three hours in studio or on location. Conversation, direction of figurine, drapery built on the dressform, atmospheric light layered slowly. Twenty-five rendered portraits and five fine art prints.

#### 03 · First Light — Newborn · Signature
> *The first portrait of a new life, painted in light.*
>
> Within the first fourteen days. Drapery, weight, hands, breath. The mother is the centre — the child, the gravity that pulls the composition toward her.

#### 04 · The Mothering — Motherhood · Signature
> *A portrait of the woman, in the act of mothering.*
>
> Studio or location. Mother and her small ones, composed slowly. Twenty rendered portraits and three fine art prints — the chapter mid-stride.

#### 05 · The Triptych — Flagship · Premium *(limited 4 per year)*
> *Three sittings across the most extraordinary year. One archive.*
>
> The Becoming, First Light, and The Mothering — composed in sequence over twelve months and bound, at close, in an archival portfolio book. A body of work, not a session.
