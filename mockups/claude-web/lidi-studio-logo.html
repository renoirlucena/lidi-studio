i · Primary Mark

For book covers, ceremonial pages, the first plate of any document. The "L" carries a calligraphic terminal; tittles on the "i" become small lozenges in Aged Gold; the "d" lifts off the page.

ii · Horizontal Lockup
Header · Watermark

For headers, signatures, watermarks. Italic mixed-case wordmark, a single Aged Gold dot for separation, and the discipline of caps.

iii · Monogram
Painter's L

A single italic L, broader of stroke than the font default; the foot extends into a flourish that lifts. The painter's signature.

iv · Wax Seal
Correspondence

A circular Aged Gold disc with a soft radial gradient — like wax cooled around the seal. The L sits in cream; the inscription circles below in mono caps.

v · Archival Stamp
Verso of every print

For the verso of every archival print. A bordered field, the wordmark in miniature, gilded inscriptions above and below. Stamped by hand where possible.

vi · Favicon
32 × 32

A simplified L on Champagne Cream, optimised for tiny rendering. The flourish becomes a single horizontal mark; legibility wins at 32 pixels.

vii · Variant Moments
Reversed · Charcoal · Gold-only

The mark holds across grounds. Cream remains the natural; charcoal is the rare ceremonial reverse; mauve is permitted only on editorial covers.
