Manifesto · MMXXVI

Motherhood is the most extraordinary chapter of a woman's life — and most of it disappears in phone rolls and pastel props.

Lidi paints with light. Twenty years behind the camera. A lifetime behind the easel.

Every session is composed: the wardrobe sculpted, the light layered, the body honored. These are not snapshots of a season — they are portraits of who you became.

Painted, not posed. Remembered, not just photographed.

About the Studio

She paints with light.
She paints with oil.

Lidi / LEE-dee / is a fine art photographer based in Anchorage, Alaska. Twenty years behind the camera. A lifetime behind the easel.

Her practice as an oil painter informs every frame: composition is built, not captured; light is layered, not found; drapery is sculpted on the dressform before it ever meets the model. The photograph is the last gesture, not the first.

The studio composes a small number of commissions each season. Maternity, newborn, motherhood, family — each rendered as a portrait, not a session.
